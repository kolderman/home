Office sound isolation (#13)

Walls
Staggered or double studs on all four office walls, with mineral wool in the cavity and two layers of ⅝" drywall with a damping compound between them. Outlets on opposite sides of a wall are offset, never back to back.
Door
Solid-core door with perimeter seals and an automatic drop seal at the bottom. The door is the weak point; a hollow door undoes the walls.
Ceiling
Mineral wool over the office ceiling and resilient channel under it, so sound doesn't travel over the walls through the attic.
Air
A lined duct with a bend in it for the supply and return, so the HVAC doesn't carry voices. With the conditioned attic this is easy to route.
Inside
Fabric or felt panels behind the TV wall and the desk soak up echo, so calls and movies sound better too.

Same method as the laundry wall (#11), so one framing detail covers both. It adds roughly $3–6k over a standard office, mostly the door and the double framing (my estimate, not a quote).

Materials

Eight materials, used everywhere, instead of a different idea in every room. Most have a Louisiana story, which is what makes a house feel rooted instead of decorated.

Light, shade and sound

Lighting
Plan it with a lighting designer, room by room, in layers: cove light in the trays and over the beams, picture lights on the art, sconces at the beds and vanities, and very few downlights. Use tunable-white fixtures (Lutron Ketra or similar) that warm up toward evening, on a central Lutron system.
Shades
Use motorized shades recessed into ceiling pockets, so nothing hangs at the top of the glass. Put sheers and blackouts in the bedrooms, and solar shades on the 16 ft glass wall.
Sound
Hide the speakers in the plaster (Sonance Invisible or similar) in the great room, lounge and primary suite. Use acoustic plaster or felt panels behind the office wall and in the great room ceiling. A big open room with hard floors gets loud fast.
Tech that disappears
It all runs back to the rack you already planned in the server room. Lighting, shades and audio join the Scrypted cameras on the same network, but the house never looks wired.
