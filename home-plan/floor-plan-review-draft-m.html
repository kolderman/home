Floor plan review, as lead architect

This is a good plan, and the pending picks make it better. Moving the primary bedroom to the pond, the cathedral living room and the guest foyer each fix a real weakness. Four things have to be fixed before draft M is built: a pocket door that can't be built, a mirror in a new doorway, the front pond against the kitchen wall, and bath 3's under-code shower if no wing option is taken. Thirteen smaller changes would make it noticeably better.

Bottom lineSay yes to the office swap, office layout 3, great room G2 and guest wing G1, but build them into draft M with the four fixes below and the suite door, powder buffer and island prep sink changes. Then it's ready for an architect's construction set.

The plan I reviewed

Draft M isn't built yet, so I drew the combination you're leaning toward on one plan: draft L, plus the primary bedroom in the office spot with office layout 3, great room G2 and guest wing G1. Everything is from the plan data, to scale. Grey outlines are furniture and fixtures, dashed lines are beams, and purple dashes are sound walls.

Fix before draft M

1. Bedroom-to-bath pocket door can't be builtThe pocket door in the bedroom's right wall (x 86, y 5.3 to 7.8) has nowhere to slide. One side is the outside corner of the house, and the other side is where the bath/dressing wall lands at y 8, with the new dressing room opening 2.7 ft beyond. A swinging door there would hit the existing dressing-room-to-bath door. Fix: Drop it. The route is bedroom → dressing room (new 3 ft opening) → bath, which is the classic suite sequence and only a few steps longer.
2. The new dressing room opening lands on the mirrorDraft L moved the full-length mirror to the dressing room's left end wall (x 86, y 10.6 to 13.8). The office swap cuts the new bedroom opening in exactly that spot. Fix: Put the mirror on the inside face of the wardrobe end panel at x 90.6, or on one wardrobe door.
3. The front pond would sit 1 ft from the kitchen wallGreat room G1/G2 fills the courtyard notch and moves the kitchen's front wall to y 62.5. The study then moves the front ecosystem pond to y 63.5 to 71.5. That puts standing water 12 in from a slab-on-grade foundation, in clay with a high water table. Fix: Keep the water edge at least 3 ft off the wall (start it at y 65.5 or later) with a gravel and planting strip, or build it as a raised, fully lined basin with the foundation waterproofed.
4. Bath 3's shower is under code in draft LDraft L still draws bath 3's shower 26 in wide. The code minimum is 30 in. Every guest wing option (G1, W4, V1) fixes it, so this only matters if draft M were built without one. Fix: Build draft M with a guest wing option. G1 gives bath 3 a 36 × 80 in shower.

Walls and structure

Walls that come out
The old office corridor and powder walls, the house's outside wall at x 106 (beam), the kitchen's notched front wall (x 52 to 68 at y 59, with its two short returns), and the old guest hall partitions, linen nook and walk-in.
New walls
Suite foyer and powder (y 27 and x 78), the office's new outside walls (x 112, plus short runs at y 16 and y 32), the kitchen's front wall at y 62.5 (new footing), and the guest wing's closet and vestibule walls.
Beams and posts
Primary bedroom glass header, 16 ft. Office beam at x 106, 16 ft. G2 ridge beam, 28 ft, on posts at (24, 48.25), (52, 34) and (52, 62.5), with footings. The x 52 timber portal. The living glass wall's moment frame. The garage door header and the trash corner beam carry over from draft L.
Sound walls
All four office walls, the primary bedroom's x 68 wall (outdoor kitchen and dining) and its y 27 wall (powder), the laundry wall, and the server wall in the guest wing.
Plumbing
Stacks well. The powder vanity backs onto the suite foyer's morning bar, both guest baths sit on the outside wall, and the kitchen and scullery sinks are both on the front wall. The island prep sink is the one new run.
HVAC
G2 removes the attic over the living room, so plan two systems (bedroom wing attic and kitchen/scullery attic) with low returns in the living room.
