What the review changes would look like

Draft H with the five open review items drawn in: a narrower island, an entry with a coat closet, a smaller stair with a closet beside it and a bigger pantry, a linen closet off the bedroom hall, and a second office window. Nothing here is decided. Draft H is unchanged.

Two review items are withdrawnOn a second look, the grill already sits on the yard side of the outdoor kitchen, and the washer and dryer are already on the garage wall, not the bedroom wall. Neither needs a change.

Proposed plan, whole house

Same footprint as draft H. Only the five areas below move.

1. Kitchen island

The island drops from 6' to 4' wide. That opens the aisle between the island and the range from about 25" to 48", so the oven door and a person can both be there. It keeps the three stools on the living side and one on the end, and loses the two on the range side, which were in the way of the cook anyway.

2. Entry and coat closet

A tiled entry about 13' × 6' inside the front doors marks the arrival, with a 4' × 4' coat closet in the corner by the kitchen wall. The potted tree moves to the dining window so it is not the first thing you walk into. The two chairs slide back a foot to clear the path to the sofa.

3. Stairs, closet and pantry

The stair goes from 10'6" × 14'6" to 7'6" × 11'6": two 3' runs, 8 treads each, and a 3'6" landing at the front. That still works for a 10' ceiling. The 3' strip beside it becomes a hall closet for coats and the vacuum, with its own door. The pantry grows from 10' deep to 13' and gets a full cabinet run on its new back wall. The under-stair storage door goes away.

4. Linen closet

A 2' × 3' linen closet in the short passage between the bedroom hall and the living room, on bedroom 3's wall. The server room door slides 16" to the right to make room. The passage stays 4'6" wide.

5. Office window

A 3' window on the porch-side wall beside the desk gives the office light from two sides. The media built-in on that wall shortens from 19' to 12'6" to leave room for it. The TV stays where it is.

What each one costs

Island
Two fewer stools and about 12 sf of counter. Nothing else moves.
Entry
About 16 sf of living room goes to the closet, and a wall meets the kitchen wall at the corner. The tile floor is optional; a rug does the same job.
Stairs
Steeper than draft H: 16 risers of about 7½" with 10½" treads, which is normal residential. The upstairs landing shrinks to match, so check it against the bonus room layout.
Linen
The passage narrows from 6'6" to 4'6" at the closet. The server door moves, nothing else does.
Office window
Less built-in shelving on the porch wall. The window faces the back porch, so it will be shaded when the porch roof is on.
